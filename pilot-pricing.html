Assumptions & formulas — how every number is computed
  • AI-handled interactions / yr = annual call volume × target containment. Containment means fully resolved by Envoz with no human handling.
  • Staff hours saved / yr = AI-handled interactions × average handle time ÷ 60.
  • Cost avoided / yr = AI-handled interactions × (your cost per human-handled call − $0.42 estimated Envoz cost per AI interaction). The $0.42 figure is illustrative demo data from the Vista Robles demo world.
  • After-hours capture value / yr = call volume × percent after-hours × containment × (handle time ÷ 60) × staff hourly cost × 50%. We assume half of after-hours calls would otherwise become next-morning voicemail triage work at your staff hourly cost; the other half we conservatively assume would be re-dialed during business hours and are already counted in cost avoided.
  • Annual platform value = cost avoided + after-hours capture value.
  • Payback period (months) = (assumed monthly pilot fee × 12) ÷ annual platform value × 12. The assumed fee follows your population input: $3,500/mo (small, under 75k), $6,750/mo (mid-size, 75k–250k — the midpoint of the $4,500–$9,000 range), or $9,000/mo (large, 250k+). Your actual pilot fee is set during discovery.
  • No value is assigned to resident satisfaction, equity coverage, or council reporting — real benefits we deliberately leave out of the math.
ℹ️ These are planning estimates for procurement conversations — not a guarantee of savings. Every city's call mix, staffing model, and after-hours volume differs; your pilot's weekly success reviews measure the real numbers.

Procurement notes

What your review team will ask — answered plainly

  • 🛡Security review packet available. Architecture overview, data-flow diagrams, and control descriptions on request. Envoz is a demonstration prototype — we keep compliance claims modest and will not assert certifications we do not hold.
  • 🏷Tenant isolation. Every rule, vocabulary entry, and case write is scoped to your city's tenant. Corrections your staff make train your configuration — not a shared model other cities inherit.
  • 📁Your data stays yours. The city owns its case data, transcripts, and configuration. Export at any time; deletion honored at contract end.
  • 🗄System of record unchanged. Overlay mode writes into your existing CRM — case numbers come from your system, never fabricated.
  • ✋Humans stay accountable. Low-confidence calls escalate to people with full context; configuration changes require city approval. Envoz protects staff from chaos — it does not replace them.
