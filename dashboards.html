Autonomy boundaries

Governed autonomy is the product. These boundaries are enforced in the platform, not just promised.

Nico CAN — autonomously
  • Monitor routing accuracy, SLA risk, equity, and language quality 24/7
  • Analyze correction trends and surge patterns across every interaction
  • Draft rules, vocabulary, and taxonomy proposals with evidence attached
  • Simulate and run sandbox tests against historical cases
  • Prepare council reports and improvement summaries
  • Create proposed configuration changes for review
Nico CANNOT
  • Silently change production routing
  • Fabricate case numbers or resolution dates — ever
  • Change the global taxonomy from one city’s correction
  • Deploy any production change without city approval
🛡Production changes require city approval — and every approval is logged to the Civic Evidence Ledger.
