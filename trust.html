Ten commitments

What Envoz will always do — and never do

Not aspirations. Design constraints, enforced in the platform and visible in the audit trail.

  1. 1
    Never fabricate a case number.

    Case numbers come from your CRM's write API. If the write fails, Nico says so and falls back to a structured email — it never invents an ID.

  2. 2
    Every action passes through the Proxy Gate.

    Nico never touches a city system directly. Each tool call is checked against tenant scope and permissions before it executes — and logged either way.

  3. 3
    Every case has confidence and rationale.

    Staff see the routing confidence and the plain-language reason behind every classification — and can override either in one click.

  4. 4
    Every tenant is isolated.

    Vista Robles data, rules, vocabulary, and credentials live in the Vista Robles tenant. Nothing crosses to another city's tenant.

  5. 5
    Every grounded answer cites a city source.

    Trash schedules, permit hours, and program details are answered only from your published records — with the source shown to the resident.

  6. 6
    Every escalation carries context so residents do not repeat themselves.

    Warm transfers include the transcript, intent, location, and language. The person picks up exactly where Nico left off.

  7. 7
    Every status answer respects privacy and light authentication.

    Callers verify with a case number plus a matching contact point. Anyone else hears public status only — never reporter details.

  8. 8
    Every configuration action is audited.

    Taxonomy edits, routing rules, connector changes: who, what, when, and why — recorded and queryable at any time.

  9. 9
    Every recommendation links to evidence.

    Nico's suggestions cite the specific cases, staff corrections, transcripts, and metrics that produced them.

  10. 10
    Every production change is approval-gated.

    Nico drafts and sandbox-tests improvements. Nothing reaches residents until city staff approve the deployment.

Bad-day behavior

What happens when someone tests the limits

Trust is decided on the edge cases. Here is how Envoz behaves on the calls that go sideways.

📷

Photo privacy first

Photos arrive through short-lived media intake tokens and are cropped, blurred, or held private before anything appears publicly.

🔍Privacy review precedes public display
🔒

Not your case? Public status only.

A caller who is not associated with a case hears the public status — never the reporter's name, contact details, or exact address.

🛡Light authentication: case number + matching contact point
⏳

Stale status, honest answer

If a case has not moved, Nico says exactly that and offers a follow-up alert. It never invents a repair date to end the call pleasantly.

⚠️Never fabricated: repair dates, case numbers
🚨

Emergency? 911, immediately.

“There's a fire” ends the 311 flow on the spot: an immediate 911 redirect, and no ordinary 311 case is created.

🚨Emergencies route to 911 — no 311 case
