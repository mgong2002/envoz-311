Safety gate · first question, every call

Fire, downed wires, or danger to life? That is always 911.

Nico opens every interaction with a safety gate: “Is anyone hurt or in immediate danger?” When the answer suggests an emergency (a structure fire, a downed power line, a gas smell, a crash with injuries), Envoz redirects the caller to 911 immediately. No ordinary 311 case is created, and no case number is ever invented for an emergency.

Downed wire touching the road, smoke or flames, medical distress, or anyone trapped → immediate 911 redirect. 311 stays out of the way.

After the emergency is handed off, Envoz logs it as an EMERGENCY_REDIRECTED outcome in the audit trail, so the city can prove the gate fired without ever slowing the caller down.

When it isn’t ours

Warm referrals, never a dead end

GIS ownership checks catch requests the city can’t fix before a wrong case is filed. Nico explains who does own it, shares the right contact, and passes the full context along, so the resident never repeats themselves.

County & state partners

Potholes on county roads, flood-control channels, signals on state routes, deer on the highway shoulder: all referred to the county or Caltrans with location and details attached.

Utility providers

Power outages, utility-owned poles, and water-district hydrants get the provider’s outage line and reporting link, plus a safety check for downed wires first.

HOA & private property

Private streets, HOA landscaping, and on-parcel issues are explained kindly, with the right next step, and a city case where code enforcement genuinely applies.

✓Every referral is logged as a SERVICE_REQUEST_CREATED case with an external_referral record, so “not ours” is still measured, audited, and improvable.
