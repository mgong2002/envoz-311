The platform

A civic intent-to-action routing brain — with voice-first intake.

Envoz 311 sits between every resident channel and your systems of record. It listens, classifies, locates, routes, and writes — and it shows its work on every decision. One call. Correctly classified. Correctly routed. With confidence.

The Envoz 311 platform is an AI voice 311 layer with three parts: multichannel intake (voice, web, SMS), a routing brain that classifies resident intent with confidence and rationale, and a 311 CRM overlay that writes structured cases into systems like SeeClickFix and Tyler — or runs standalone where no CRM exists. Two deployment modes

Overlay your CRM — or run standalone until you have one

Cities do not need another CRM. Envoz arms the one you have; where none exists, it stands in with a lightweight case surface. Either way: no rip-and-replace.

Envoz writes into the systems you already run

Residents and staff keep the tools they know. Envoz answers the phone, classifies and routes the request, then creates the case through your CRM's own API — so your CRM issues the case number and remains the single source of truth.

Engineered for the bad days

What Envoz does when things go wrong

Any platform looks good on a sunny Tuesday. Cities should judge 311 systems by their failure behavior — so here is ours, in the open.

🔌

The CRM write fails

Envoz retries with an idempotency key — no duplicate cases. If the CRM stays down, the request falls back to a structured email to the department, and the resident is told the truth about what happened.

🗄Write to SeeClickFix
🔁Retry ×3 · idempotency key attached
📧Structured-email fallback delivered
✓Fallback delivered & audit-logged · no duplicate case, no false confirmation

Never fabricated: Envoz only confirms a case after a system of record confirms it first.

◔

Confidence is low

Below the routing threshold, Envoz does not guess. It warm-transfers to a person with the full context packet — summary, transcript, resolved location, and callback number — so the resident never repeats their story.

◔41% confidence → warm transfer, not a wrong route

Low-confidence patterns are logged to the CEL, so the same gap gets a proposed fix — reviewed and approved by your staff.
