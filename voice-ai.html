Trust is what happens on a bad day

Bad-day behaviors, by design

Anyone can demo a sunny-day call. These are the rules Envoz follows when the call is messy, the data is stale, or the caller isn't who you expect.

🕰

Stale status

If the CRM hasn't posted an update, Nico says exactly that. It never invents repair dates or crew ETAs — "I don't have a new update yet, and I won't guess" is an honest answer.

🔊

Noisy audio

Ask once, then move on. If a field is still unclear, Nico marks it uncertain, lowers the confidence score, and escalates with the recording context — instead of interrogating the caller.

📷

Photo privacy

Resident photos are cropped, and faces and license plates blurred, before anything appears on a public map. Originals stay tenant-scoped and access-logged.

🛡

Non-associated caller

Someone checking on a case they didn't file gets public status only — never the reporter's name, callback number, or exact address.
